# "VEIL" — full lyrics

**Length:** 3:00 · **Tempo:** 120 BPM (4/4, one bar = 2s — every 8s video clip = exactly 4 bars)
**Key:** F minor → final chorus lifts to G minor · **Vocal:** female alto — breathy and close in the verses, belted in the chorus

The signature move is written into the chorus itself: *"one hand over my face and I'm — gone"* — a palm
sweep down across the face, with a full beat of silence before "gone". That gesture + pause is the
loopable, imitable moment the whole video is built around.
