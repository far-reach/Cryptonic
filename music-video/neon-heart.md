# "NEON HEART" — cyberpunk metal love story

**Length:** 3:00 · **Tempo:** 120 BPM industrial-metal groove (4/4, bar = 2s) · **Key:** E minor,
final choruses +2 → F# minor · **Vocals:** harsh processed shouts (verses) vs. clean female
(choruses) — the classic beauty-and-beast metal contrast, rendered with espeak-ng + DSP.

**Story:** a human courier loves NV-7 — an android the Halcyon corporation has scheduled for a
memory wipe at dawn. He breaks into the wipe facility; her ECG flatlines; the song itself stops
on a flatline tone — then slams back: **ALIVE!** The recurring signature: every chorus cuts to a
heart-monitor blip for one beat before the word "**BEAT!**" lands.

Produced by the same self-contained pipeline as VEIL (see `README.md`):
`src/synth_neon_heart.py` → `neon_heart.wav`, `src/neon_heart.html` (23 deterministic cyberpunk
scenes: rain, neon kanji signs, the circuit-heart ignition between interlocked hands, the STAY!
crowd, the wipe slab flatline, the dawn ending), `src/capture.mjs` → `neon_heart_master.mp4`.
