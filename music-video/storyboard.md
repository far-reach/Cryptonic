# "VEIL" — shot-by-shot storyboard

23 shots × 8 seconds (4 bars each at 120 BPM) = 184s of footage, trimmed to a 3:00 master in the
edit. Master is **16:9**; shots 5, 7, 17–19 get **9:16** reframes as short-form cutdowns.

## World and character (paste-ready consistency blocks)

Every prompt below references these two blocks verbatim — generate a character sheet first
(character-sheet workflow → `nano_banana_pro`), then pass that image as a reference to every
character shot (`seedance_2_5` multimodal reference consistency).

**CHARACTER** — *"VEIL dancer": androgynous woman, early 20s, dark slicked-back hair, sharp calm
face, matte black streetwear (cropped jacket, wide trousers, black boots), wearing an iridescent
translucent veil-cloak that shifts pearl/violet/gold as it moves. No logos, no text on clothing.*

**WORLD** — *"the Glass City": a hyper-modern city at golden hour where every building, wall and
vehicle is transparent glass; above every pedestrian's head floats a soft holographic ticker of
glowing numbers (their money, visible to everyone); thin luminous data-ribbons trail behind people
as they walk. Cinematic, photoreal, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, no on-screen text.*

**THE MOVE** — *she sweeps an open palm slowly down across her face; where the palm passes, her
face dissolves into golden shimmer and drifting light-moths; the number ticker above her head
shatters into hundreds of tiny glowing moths.*

## Sync rules for the edit

- Every cut lands on a downbeat (the 8s grid guarantees this at 120 BPM).
- Shot 5 and shot 17 must align THE MOVE's palm-pass with the beat of silence before "gone".
- Shots 1→2 and 22→23 crossfade; every other transition is a hard cut.
- Grade: golden-hour warmth for city shots, cold blue for shots 8–10, amber for 13–15,
  aurora pastels for 19–21.
