Time-stamping authorities (RFC 3161)

Each enabled TSA receives only the 32-byte SHA-256 digest of the evidence manifest plus a random nonce — never the page content. Enabling two independent TSAs gives your evidence a second, redundant time anchor.

    Saving with a custom TSA asks for permission to contact that host.

    Privacy

    Everything — archiving, hashing, report generation, bundling — happens locally in your browser. The only network traffic WebNotary generates is the timestamp request described above. There is no account, no telemetry, and no server.
