# Ten Vitalik Ideas Still Waiting for Builders

Across roughly 95 posts on vitalik.eth.limo between January 2021 and October 8, 2026, about two dozen propose something concrete, and the one with the most money-making potential that nobody has yet built at scale is a **compliant, confidential stablecoin payment rail with real distribution**: the cryptographic parts Vitalik asked for (proof-of-innocence pools, stealth addresses, a wallet privacy SDK) all exist, but the largest compliant-privacy protocol holds **~$9M of TVL against a $300.9B stablecoin market** moving **more than $90 trillion a year** ([L2BEAT](https://l2beat.com/privacy/projects/privacy-pools); [The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/10/02/stablecoin-market-reaches-300-9b-as-tron-gains-5b-in-q3/); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/stablecoin-supply-hits-290-billion-annual-transfers-exceed-90-trillion)), and on October 5, 2026 FinCEN withdrew the mixer and unhosted-wallet rules that had chilled the category since 2023 ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/fincen-drops-crypto-mixing-proposal-as-backlash-kills-rule/)). Behind it, ranked by the size of the adjacent proven market multiplied by how empty the field still is, come a **liquidation-free, options-based credit design with zero implementations** against ~$30B of lending TVL; an **approval-gated, spend-capped self-sovereign AI stack** that Vitalik hand-rolled in April 2026 and that venture capital began funding only in the first week of October; **AI-made long-tail information markets and hedging baskets** riding a prediction-market industry now valued above $60B; and **AI-native formal verification for production code**, where ~$500M has gone to math-proving startups while smart-contract verification still runs on grants. The pattern across all ten is the same: the base layer Vitalik described has been commoditized (smart accounts, attestations, prediction exchanges, proof-of-reserves), while the trust-minimizing or privacy-preserving layer he actually cared about has no revenue model and therefore no builder. The July–October 2026 quarter did not add a single new product proposal to the blog (its three posts were cryptography explainers plus a novel), but it delivered the strongest catalysts in years for ideas #1 and #3. The reader's own repository, VEIL, is a direct attempt at #1; the caveat is that the unsolved part of #1 is distribution and regulator acceptance, not primitives.

## How the ranking was built, and what it leaves out

The ranking weighs four things: the size of the adjacent market that already pays (stablecoin flows, lending TVL, prediction-market revenue, security spend); how undeveloped the specific thing Vitalik asked for remains as of October 8, 2026 (players, funding, live metrics); whether a near-term catalyst opens a window (a regulatory withdrawal, a hack, a deadline); and whether a plausible revenue model exists at all. Several of Vitalik's most-repeated wishes score badly on the last criterion and therefore sit low or outside the list despite being wide open, because "unbuilt" and "worth building for money" are different questions. Deep funding, for example, has run a $250k pilot and a $350k Gitcoin round that attracted **~500 evaluations and just $1,500 of model-builder trades** ([Gitcoin gov forum](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/deep-funding-gg24-web3-tooling-and-infra-round/25040)); it is unbuilt, but there is no customer.

Two scope notes matter. First, the research behind this report was search-only (the blog's text could not be fetched directly), so several 2021–2024 post summaries rest on prior knowledge of the posts rather than retrieved text and should be checked against the GitHub mirror before quoting ([vbuterin/blog](https://github.com/vbuterin/blog)). Second, in 2026 Vitalik's actionable proposals moved off the blog: the index for 2026 shows six essays (April 2 on his local-LLM setup, May 18 on formal verification, June 29 / July 28 / August 21 on obfuscation, September 27 on "the cryptographic world computer") and nothing in January–March or October ([site index](https://vitalik.eth.limo/categories/general.html)), while the options-based DeFi design went to ethresear.ch and the EIP-8288 privacy-cost proposal, the lattice-cryptography warning, and the "corposlop" prediction-market critique went to X ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/vitalik-buterin-options-based-defi-replace-liquidation-driven-debt); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/377857/vitalik-buterin-pushes-ethereum-plan-to-slash-quantum-safe-privacy-costs); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-02-15-polymarket-investor-vitalik-buterin-says-prediction-markets-need-to-stop-catering-to-dumb-opinions-389984)). Where an X or ethresear.ch item sharpens a blog idea, it is cited as such; every ranked entry is anchored in at least one blog post.

## The ranked list: ten unbuilt ideas by money-making potential

### 1. A compliant, confidential stablecoin payment rail with real distribution

**Source posts.** "Some ways to use ZK-SNARKs for privacy" (June 15, 2022) introduced coin mixing with a "proof of innocence"; "An incomplete guide to stealth addresses" (January 20, 2023) specified meta-addresses and flagged gas-payment and scanning as open problems; "What I would love to see in a wallet" (December 3, 2024) asked for privacy by default via stealth addresses, privacy pools and shielded balances; "Why I support privacy" (April 14, 2025) listed privacy pools with compliance proofs and wallet-default privacy as the tools he wants; and "Low-risk defi can be for Ethereum what search was for Google" (September 21, 2025) put payments and fully collateralized stablecoins at the center of Ethereum's sustainable business ([using_snarks](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2022/06/15/using_snarks.html); [stealth](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2023/01/20/stealth.html); [wallets](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2024/12/03/wallets.html); [privacy](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/04/14/privacy.html); [low_risk_defi](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/09/21/low_risk_defi.html)). His September 23, 2026 talk sharpened the framing for the AI era: public chains are "Twitter for your bank account," pseudonymity is insufficient once AI can link wallets to people, and the design question becomes "Who is allowed to see this?" ([Cryptotimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/23/vitalik-buterin-warns-ai-is-changing-the-blockchain-privacy-debate/)).

**The concrete idea** is a stablecoin transfer that reveals nothing to the public (sender, receiver, amount) yet lets the sender prove membership in a clean association set and lets a chosen auditor decrypt via a viewing key, wired into a wallet people already use.

**How developed it is.** Every primitive exists; none has volume. Privacy Pools, the Vitalik-coauthored proof-of-innocence design, launched on mainnet in March 2025, had processed **$6M across 1,500+ users** by its $3.5M seed in November 2025 (Starbloom, Coinbase Ventures, Balaji Srinivasan), and now shows **~$9.25M TVL, 7,480 lifetime deposits and 588 deposits in the trailing 30 days** ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/0xbow-raises-usd3-5-million-to-expand-privacy-pools); [L2BEAT](https://l2beat.com/privacy/projects/privacy-pools)). For scale, Tornado Cash's lifetime volume exceeded $7B ([bex.co](https://bex.co/blog/2026/04/12/0xbow-privacy-compliance-defi-fatf-travel-rule)). Railgun, the most-used shielded DeFi system, reports ~$100M TVL but **~83% is staked governance tokens; genuinely shielded value is ~$15.8M**, and its 0.25% shield/unshield fee produced **$1.24M of gross revenue in Q4 2025**; it deployed to Base on September 1, 2026 ([Phemex](https://phemex.com/blogs/what-is-railgun-rail-onchain-privacy-protocol); [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/protocol/railgun); [L2BEAT Railgun](https://l2beat.com/privacy/projects/railgun)). The best-funded "private stablecoin" startup, Payy, raised a **$6M seed in March 2026** (FirstMark) and claims 100,000+ users and **~$130M annualized volume** ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/395106/stablecoin-startup-payy-funding-private-transactions); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/payy-raises-6m-seed-to-build-private-stablecoin-payments-on-zero-knowledge-rails/)). Umbra has processed >350,000 stealth transactions since 2021 but its team is "moving forward without clear funding sources" ([ScopeLift](https://scopelift.co/blog/umbra-2025-in-review-and-the-year-ahead)). The Ethereum Foundation's Kohaku wallet SDK shipped at **v0.0.1-alpha on May 25, 2026** with Railgun integration working and Privacy Pools wrappers "in active development"; no consumer wallet has shipped on it ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/ethereum-foundation-kohaku-sdk-privacy-wallet-integration-bb4t52)). Aztec's private L2 went live with Alpha V5 on July 21, 2026 and six days later disclosed a critical proving-system bug that let invalid transactions pass verification, its second such bug ([Aztec](https://aztec.network/blog/alpha-v5-proving-system-vulnerability)). Circle's Arc mainnet launched September 16, 2026 with confidential transfers "still in development," amount-only and TEE-based ([Circle](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-launches-arc-mainnet-an-economic-operating-system-for-the-internet); [Everstake](https://everstake.com/resources/blog/arcs-opt-in-privacy-selective-shielding-built-in-auditability)). MetaMask has no native private-transfer feature, only a third-party privacy Snap ([cryptonews.net](https://cryptonews.net/news/defi/32803278/)). Native L1 shielded transfers (EIP-8182) are a draft with no scheduled fork ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/eip-8182-ethereum-hegota-privacy-transfers/)).

**Why the gap remains.** Demand for privacy as an *asset* has been validated loudly: Zcash's shielded pool reached **~$7.0B (29% of supply)**, Grayscale's ZCSH ETF passed $500M of AUM within two weeks of its August 25 launch, and the Winklevoss twins filed a competing ETF on October 6 ([ZecStats](https://zecstats.org/shielded); [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001720265/000119312526385317/zcsh-ex99_1.htm); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-06-winklevoss-files-spot-zcash-etf-wink-417826)). But privacy as a *payment product* has not been, because the people who own distribution (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Phantom, Circle) have not turned it on, and compliance is the hard half: wallet-level KYC that blocks analytics linkage is "a narrow line to walk" under the FATF Travel Rule ([cryptorank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/15c85-payy-privacy-stablecoin-seed-funding)). The optics cut both ways in the quarter: North Korea–attributed hackers moved ~$3.8M of the $387.5M Bitget haul into Zcash's shielded pool ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitget-breach-zcash-shielded-laundering/)). And the clock runs: the EU's AMLR bans CASP-served anonymous accounts and privacy coins from July 2027, though self-custodial peer-to-peer transfers are out of scope ([thirdweb](https://blog.thirdweb.com/eu-privacy-coin-ban-2027-what-the-amlr-means-for-web3-builders/)).

**The monetizable opportunity.** The US window is the most open it has been since 2022: FinCEN withdrew both the 2023 mixer rule and the 2020 unhosted-wallet rule on October 5, citing their "chilling effect on legitimate on-chain financial activity," and the Roman Storm retrial slid to April 2027 ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-treasury-withdraws-crypto-surveillance-rules/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/tornado-cash-co-founder-roman-storm-retrial-pushed-to-april-2027/)). The product is a merchant- and consumer-grade confidential USDC/USDT rail with built-in proof-of-innocence and Travel-Rule-compatible selective disclosure, embedded in a wallet or neobank that already has users; the arithmetic is forgiving, since a 10-basis-point fee on one-thousandth of annual stablecoin transfer volume (~$90B) would be ~$90M a year, more than seventy times Railgun's current run rate. A second, smaller but defensible business is the **association-set provider** role from the Privacy Pools paper: no standalone vendor of curated, regulator-accepted association sets was found anywhere in the research. The distribution channels are forming now, with EM stablecoin neobanks raising at scale (Fasset at a **$1B valuation**, Yellow Card's $40M, MiniPay's 16M+ wallets) and none of them offering confidentiality ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sbi-fasset-round-1b-valuation); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/08/05/yellow-card-raises-usd40-million-to-link-banks-to-stablecoin-processing); [Opera](https://press.opera.com/2026/06/23/minipay-visa-debit-card/)).

### 2. Liquidation-free, options-based synthetic assets and credit

**Source posts.** "Two thought experiments to evaluate automated stablecoins" (May 25, 2022) asked whether a design can wind down safely and pay negative interest; "Low-risk defi" (September 21, 2025) asked for sound synthetic assets and savings products free of governance-token and oracle risk ([stable](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2022/05/25/stable.html); [low_risk_defi](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/09/21/low_risk_defi.html)). The concrete mechanism arrived on ethresear.ch on June 1, 2026: mint a (P, N) pair from 1 ETH with strike S and maturity M, redeemable any time for 1 ETH; at maturity P pays min(1, S/x) and N pays max(0, 1 − S/x), so payoffs sum to one and "there is no possibility of liquidation," which also lets the system tolerate slow, prediction-market-style oracles ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/vitalik-buterin-options-based-defi-replace-liquidation-driven-debt); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/06/01/ethereum-s-vitalik-buterin-is-rethinking-how-defi-handles-market-crashes)).

**How developed it is.** Not at all: **no deployed implementation, launch date or team was found**; the nearest primitives are Panoptic's perpetual options and Opyn's Squeeth, neither of which does this ([CoinMarketCap Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/%20vitalik-buterin-synthetic-assets-proposal)). Vitalik said on June 13 that experimentation was "already happening," but nothing has surfaced on-chain ([Memeburn](https://memeburn.com/vitalik-buterin-says-options-based-defi-is-already-happening/)). Meanwhile the incumbent, liquidation-based model is enormous and visibly fragile. Aave holds **~$19.45B** and Morpho **~$11.47B** ([FRNT Financial](https://frnt.substack.com/p/reviewing-defi-metrics-c4b)); on April 18, 2026 the Kelp DAO bridge exploit minted ~$292M of unbacked rsETH, 89,567 of which sat as Aave collateral, forcing market freezes, modeled losses of **$123M–$230M**, and a one-day Aave TVL drop from ~$22–26B to ~$14–15B ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/04/20/aave-could-face-up-to-usd230-million-in-losses-after-kelp-dao-bridge-exploit-triggers-defi-chaos)). Ethena's synthetic dollar, the other "low-risk" alternative, shrank **65% from its $14.8B peak to under $5B** and ended all incentives on September 30 ([Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/ethenas-staked-usde-stablecoin/); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/ethena-ends-usde-token-incentives/)). Q3 2026 hack losses hit **$1.25B**, with bridge and peg failures accounting for 48.9% of DeFi losses ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/defillama-250-crypto-hacks-2026/)).

**Why the gap remains.** The design has honest costs that Vitalik stated up front: periodic rebalancing with slippage and a **1–4%/year drift**, so it is "not a precise accounting stablecoin" ([Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/vitalik-buterin-proposes-options-based-defi-to-end-forced-liquidations-and-the-real-time-oracle-problem/)). Incumbents have no reason to cannibalize liquidation-based books, and the idea is four months old.

**The monetizable opportunity.** A protocol charging mint/redeem/rebalance fees on a liquidation-free synthetic (an ETH-denominated "stable-ish" asset or leveraged side) can sell safety into exactly the regulated front ends that are scaling "boring" DeFi: Coinbase has originated **>$2.3B of Morpho-powered loans to ~53,000 borrowers** and now offers fixed-rate loans and DeFi Earn in Brazil and Canada ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-05-12-coinbase-solana-loans-morpho-borrow-100000-400980); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-09-22-coinbase-fixed-rate-bitcoin-loans-morpho-midnight-416050)). The GENIUS Act's issuer yield ban, effective by January 18, 2027, is steering retail yield into exchange-reward and DeFi-vault channels rather than issuer interest, which is a tailwind for any vault-shaped product that can credibly claim no liquidation risk ([Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/occ-proposes-comprehensive-rulemaking-to-implement-the-genius-act); [Tazapay](https://tazapay.com/blog/genius-act-one-year-later-stablecoin-rules-2026)). It ranks second rather than first because its market, while large, is a protocol-fee business against a wary user base that just watched a "low-risk" synthetic lose two-thirds of its supply.

### 3. The approval-gated, spend-capped self-sovereign AI stack

**Source posts.** "The promise and challenges of crypto + AI applications" (January 30, 2024) wanted AI as a wallet interface with adversarial-ML risk managed; "My self-sovereign / local / private / secure LLM setup" (April 2, 2026) described the stack he actually runs: Qwen3.5:35B on a laptop GPU at ~90 tokens/second, NixOS declarative config, per-task bubblewrap sandboxes rooted to whitelisted directories, an open-sourced messaging daemon in which the AI can read Signal and email but every outbound message needs human approval ("human + LLM 2-of-2"), and a recommendation that wallet and agent builders **cap autonomous spending at $100/day**, citing data that ~15% of agent skills contain malicious instructions ([cryptoai](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2024/01/30/cryptoai.html); [secure_llms](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/04/02/secure_llms.html); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/363128/ethereu-founder-vitalik-buterin-details-private-secure-ai-setup); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/vitalik-buterin-self-sovereign-llm-ethereum-ai/)). In September he published a follow-up experiment routing stripped-down prompts from a local model to remote models over Tor and called local AI "nearly ready" while saying wallets "still cannot trust it alone" ([Cryptorank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/72c6b-ethereum-cofounder-vitalik-says-local-ai-is-nearly-ready-but-crypto-wallets-still-cannot-trust-it-alone)).

**How developed it is.** The stack decomposes into local hardware, a sandboxed OS, private remote fallback, and the 2-of-2 approval gate; only the first and third have products. On **October 5, 2026 Ghost AI raised an $11M a16z-led seed** for a $3,499 subscription-free local-agent appliance whose first batch sold out within hours ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/05/ai-agent-hardware-startup-ghost-led-by-its-19-year-old-founder-raises-11m/)). On **October 1 the EF and Open Anonymity Project launched zkAPI on mainnet**, implementing the Vitalik–Crapis private-inference-payment design: a prepaid on-chain vault issues a ZK-authorized, dollar-capped API key that lives only in device memory, with a local OpenAI/Ollama-compatible client ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-10-01-ethereum-foundation-launches-zkapi-417504)). The approval gate, by contrast, exists only as configuration: OpenClaw, the agent Vitalik criticized, has exec approvals but ships a "YOLO" preset that disables them, and Trend Micro notes it "does not enforce a mandatory human-in-the-loop mechanism" by default ([OpenClaw docs](https://docs.openclaw.ai/tools/exec-approvals); [Trend Micro](https://www.trendmicro.com/en_us/research/26/b/what-openclaw-reveals-about-agentic-assistants.html)). The demand side is already live and already leaking: agents have made **230M+ x402 payments worth $54M+**, but daily volume fell **93% from January to September**, much of it meme-coin farming, which says agents transact constantly and nobody yet governs what they may spend ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/developer-platform/discover/launches/c4a-equities-x402); [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/agentic-payment-rail-shows-volume-decline); [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/x402-agentic-payments-adoption/)).

**Why the gap remains.** Vitalik's setup is a power-user artifact (NixOS, bubblewrap, a custom daemon), and no vendor has packaged the policy layer. Cloudflare's August 2026 agent wallets, with owner-set allowances and seller allow-lists, and World's AgentKit are the nearest pieces, but each is tied to one vendor's rail ([Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2026/cloudflare-gives-ai-agents-an-identity-and-a-wallet/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/17/world-launches-tool-to-verify-humans-behind-ai-shopping-agents/)).

**The monetizable opportunity.** Agent security is the hottest adjacent category of the quarter: AIR ($50M, Sequoia), Reco ($55M, which found 21,000 unknown agents at one Fortune 100), Comp AI ($34M) and Catena Labs ($30M) all closed in 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/air-raises-50m-to-help-companies-vet-the-skills-and-add-ons-ai-agents-use/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/reco-raises-55m-as-ai-agent-security-startups-crowd-the-market/); [Coinbase Ventures](https://www.coinbase.com/ventures/news)). The product that nobody sells is the consumer/prosumer version: an agent runtime or wallet in which outbound messages and payments are 2-of-2 gated by default, spend caps are policy rather than prompt, and remote inference goes through zkAPI-style anonymous payment. Vitalik also asked, in the same post, for Ethereum-specific fine-tuned models for transaction verification and contract auditing; none was found from the EF or anyone else ([PANews](https://www.panewslab.com/en/articles/a435e93e-8c12-486e-9278-190328ad890f)).

### 4. AI-made long-tail information markets and hedging baskets

**Source posts.** "Prediction Markets: Tales from the Election" (February 18, 2021) diagnosed mispricing from scarce capital and poor UX; "From prediction markets to info finance" (November 9, 2024) reframed markets as information-elicitation tools for governance, science and news, with AI agents as cheap participants that make tiny markets viable ([election](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2021/02/18/election.html); [infofinance](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2024/11/09/infofinance.html)). On February 14, 2026 he, a Polymarket investor, warned that markets were "over-converging" on sports and short-term crypto bets, called the trajectory "corposlop," and specified an alternative: regional price indices for food, housing and transport, with a personal AI assistant building a tailored portfolio of positions that hedges one's expected expenses, potentially replacing stablecoins and fiat as the savings instrument ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-02-15-polymarket-investor-vitalik-buterin-says-prediction-markets-need-to-stop-catering-to-dumb-opinions-389984); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/358165/vitalik-buterin-hedging-on-prediction-markets-could-replace-fiat-currency)).

**How developed it is.** The base layer is the most hyper-developed thing in this report: Kalshi is finalizing a round at **~$40B** on **$60.7B of September notional volume** and reported >$1.5B of annualized revenue at its $22B round, and Polymarket is raising ~$1B at **$21B** ([MarketScreener/Bloomberg](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/kalshi-finalizing-funding-round-at-about-40-billion-valuation-ce785ad3da88f623); [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/prediction-markets/volume/kalshi/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/options/articles/kalshi-raises-1-billion-series-113439811.html); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/01/trump-jr-s-firm-leads-usd1-billion-polymarket-raise-at-usd21-billion-value-report)). AI *trading* agents are productized (Olas's Polystrat since February; Public's agents on Kalshi since September 24) ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/03/15/ai-agents-are-quietly-rewriting-prediction-market-trading); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/24/public-ai-trading-agents-prediction-markets-kalshi-tie-up/)). But **no platform where AI agents create and price markets on arbitrary questions was found**; Kalshi's AI only fixes contract wording internally, and Meta's AI-driven "Arena" is unlaunched and "may never ship" ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/economy/markets/2026/kalshi-create-ai-agent-to-smooth-prediction-market-contracts/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/23/mark-zuckerberg-wants-meta-to-launch-its-own-prediction-market/)). Decision markets, the governance leg, exist in production only at MetaDAO, whose **~$2.4M of cumulative revenue** is really ICO-launchpad fees; Butter's Optimism pilot forecast a $239M TVL gain against $31M realized ([Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/rangers-ico-metadao); [Optimism gov forum](https://gov.optimism.io/t/futarchy-v1-preliminary-findings/10062)). Nothing implements the expense-basket idea; Kalshi's October 2 filing of a perpetual-style oil contract is the nearest step toward hedging instruments ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/markets/prediction-markets/)).

**Why the gap remains.** Liquidity in the long tail is thin by definition, sports pay the bills (Pew found volume doubled between May and July, "largely driven by sports"), and the US legal position is a circuit split: the Ninth and Sixth Circuits sided with states in August and September while the Third sided with Kalshi, and 44 states told the CFTC it has no authority over sports contracts ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/09/23/prediction-markets-trading-volume-doubled-between-may-and-july-largely-driven-by-sports/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/appeals-court-rules-against-prediction-markets-tees-up-scotus-fight.html); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/44-states-say-cftc-has-no-authority-over-sports-prediction-markets.html)).

**The monetizable opportunity.** Two products sit on top of an industry that already pays. First, a market-creation API: generate, resolve and seed micro-markets with agent liquidity, sold to media (odds licensing is now standard, from CNN and Dow Jones to Substack, but data is being given away rather than sold) and to enterprises that want internal forecasts ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/media-platforms/journalism/polymarket-kalshi-cnn-cnbc-dow-jones/); [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/02/polymarket-says-journalism-is-better-when-its-backed-by-live-markets-does-anyone-know-what-that-means/)). Second, the hedging basket Vitalik spelled out, which is less a prediction market than a consumer savings product and could be offered inside any exchange or neobank. Both avoid the sports-gambling legal fight entirely.

### 5. AI-native formal verification for production code

**Source posts.** "My response to AI 2027" (July 10, 2025) named formal verification as cyber-defense; "The importance of full-stack openness and verifiability" (September 24, 2025) extended it to hardware; "A shallow dive into formal verification" (May 18, 2026) called AI-assisted machine-checkable proofs the "final form" of secure software development, with humans writing specs and AI writing Lean and proofs, while conceding proofs miss side channels and bad assumptions ([2027](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/07/10/2027.html); [openness](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/09/24/openness.html); [fv](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/05/18/fv.html); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/05/18/vitalik-buterin-says-ai-formal-verification-could-actually-make-crypto-much-more-secure)). On September 16 he said defenders win if they use AI for FV, that "defining 'secure' is the hard part," and that ~90% of his net worth sits in crypto on that bet ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/378544/ethereum-vitalik-buterin-ai-crypto-security)).

**How developed it is.** Capital has arrived, but for mathematics: Axiom Math raised **$200M at $1.6B** in March 2026 after solving all twelve Putnam 2025 problems, and Harmonic raised **$120M at $1.45B** (Nvidia joined in January), both pre-revenue and aimed at Lean proofs of theorems ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/12/verifiable-ai-startup-axiom-raises-200m-prove-ai-generated-code-safe-use/); [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/01/15/nvidia-harmonic-ai-math-accuracy)). Blockchain FV, by contrast, runs on grants and audit fees: the EF's **$20M Verified-zkEVM program** anchors Veridise (LLZK v1.0, April 8, 2026), Nethermind and Certora, whose AI Composer verifies generated code and whose Prover is open source; none of the three shows a 2026 raise, and no FV funding round at all was found in July–October ([EF zkEVM](https://zkevm.ethereum.foundation/); [Certora](https://www.certora.com/blog/certora-goes-open-source); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/certora-vs-veridise)).

**Why the gap remains.** Specs, not proofs, are the bottleneck, and specs do not scale with GPU count. The money went where benchmarks are clean (Olympiad problems) rather than where customers are (wallets, bridges, provers). Aztec's July bug was found by internal AI-assisted auditing, which shows the demand, and the fact that it shipped at all shows the gap ([Aztec](https://aztec.network/blog/alpha-v5-proving-system-vulnerability)).

**The monetizable opportunity.** Security spend after **$2.68B of 2026 hack losses** is non-discretionary, and the EF's August 10 roadmap states that "full FV of everything is only possible because of modern AI" ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/defillama-250-crypto-hacks-2026/); [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323885/20260811/buterin-names-seven-ethereum-roadmap-items-previously-impossible-build.htm)). The unbuilt product is spec-first, AI-native verification of production contracts, wallets, provers and clients sold as a continuous service rather than a one-off audit, with the EF, L2s and insurers as early customers. It ranks fifth because its revenue today is grant-shaped and because the well-funded math players could pivot down into it.

### 6. The trust-minimized wallet layer: keystore rollups, EIL, a Kohaku consumer wallet, quantum-ready keys

**Source posts.** "Why we need wide adoption of social recovery wallets" (January 11, 2021), "The Three Transitions" (June 9, 2023), "Deeper dive on cross-L2 reading for wallets" (June 20, 2023) and "What I would love to see in a wallet" (December 3, 2024) together specify a wallet with guardian recovery, a keystore readable from every L2, seamless cross-chain sends without bridging UX, light-client verification and privacy by default ([recovery](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2021/01/11/recovery.html); [three_transitions](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2023/06/09/three_transitions.html); [deeperdive](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2023/06/20/deeperdive.html); [wallets](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2024/12/03/wallets.html)). On October 7–8, 2026 he added a new ask from X: AI may break lattice cryptography within ~2 years, so prefer hash-based signatures and keep funds in fresh never-sent-from addresses, "but don't rush anything" ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-10-08-vitalik-buterin-warns-hasty-wallet-migrations-418007)).

**How developed it is.** The generic account layer is commoditized: **>27M EIP-7702 delegated accounts**, Safe at **63M accounts securing $27B+**, Ready with 2M downloads ([Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/research/ethereum-eip-7702-code-delegation-wallets); [Safe](https://safefoundation.org/smart-contracts); [Ready](https://www.ready.co/faq)). The trust-minimizing pieces are all pre-mainnet: Base's minimal keystore rollup is documented but not deployed, Axiom's is a private devnet on Sepolia, Functor's is "testnet → mainnet" ([Base](https://blog.base.dev/exploring-the-keystore); [Axiom](https://keystore.axiom.xyz/); [functor.sh](https://www.functor.sh/)); the EF's Ethereum Interop Layer has been on testnet since November 2025 with no mainnet date found ([EF blog](https://blog.ethereum.org/2025/11/18/eil)); Helios still warns against use as a main RPC ([a16z/helios](https://github.com/a16z/helios)); and Kohaku's reference extension is "targeted at advanced users, not a final product" with Ambire "preparing" an implementation ([Coinlive](https://www.coinlive.com/news/a-detailed-analysis-of-kohaku-s-end-to-end-privacy-solution-and-user); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/kohaku-sdk-wallet-privacy-integration/)). The commoditized layer has its own problem: a 2026 preprint found **63% of observed 7702 delegations point to malicious contracts** with $2.36M in confirmed losses ([IACR 2026/2060](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2060)). **No 2026 wallet funding round built around Vitalik's spec was found.**

**Why the gap remains.** The trust-minimization layer has no token and no fee, so public-goods teams build it slowly and venture teams skip it; adoption is gated on wallet vendors, who have just been handed a reason (malicious delegations) to be cautious.

**The monetizable opportunity.** A wallet company that assembles the spec (Kohaku privacy, keystore-based recovery, EIL sends) and monetizes the way incumbents do, through in-wallet flows, would own the distribution that idea #1 lacks, which is why these two ideas are natural to combine. Two narrower wedges have immediate demand: a vetted-delegate registry with insurance for 7702, and hash-based-signature migration tooling for the "bunker mode" Vitalik just described ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/380503/ai-break-crypto-encryption-ethereum-vitalik-buterin-bunker-mode)).

### 7. Turnkey ZK proof of solvency for mid-tier exchanges and custodians

**Source post.** "Having a safe CEX: proof of solvency and beyond" (November 19, 2022) specified Merkle-sum proofs of liabilities plus proof of asset control, ZK versions that hide balances, user-verifiable inclusion, and, "beyond," exchanges run as validiums with user-controlled withdrawals ([proof_of_solvency](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2022/11/19/proof_of_solvency.html)).

**How developed it is.** The asset side is routine: Binance publishes monthly reports (45th in August 2026, with zk-SNARK liability checks since 2023), OKX uses zk-STARKs, Kraken and Bybit publish audited Merkle trees ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/binance-proof-of-reserves-august-2026/); [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/proof-of-reserves-crypto-exchanges/)). But most systems prove only assets, Chainlink's proof-of-solvency framework has no live exchange deployment, and the September 24 **Bitget breach ($387.5M via compromised backend systems, no key theft)** plus Zondacrypto's ~4,500 BTC it could not access show that snapshots do not prevent losses ([DeFi Planet](https://defi-planet.com/2026/04/proof-of-reserve-2-0-how-crypto-exchanges-are-proving-solvency-in-a-new-transparency-era/); [Chainlink](https://blog.chain.link/proof-of-solvency/); [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/bitget-hack-north-korea-attribution/); [DailyCoin](https://dailycoin.com/proof-of-reserves-crypto-exchanges-limitations)). **No dedicated ZK proof-of-solvency vendor funding or product launch was found in 2026.**

**Why the gap remains.** Solvency proofs are voluntary marketing for exchanges, while the regulatory pull (GENIUS's monthly PCAOB-examined reserve reports) lands on stablecoin issuers, so no one has been forced to buy ([Paul Hastings](https://www.paulhastings.com/insights/crypto-policy-tracker/the-genius-act-a-comprehensive-guide-to-us-stablecoin-regulation)).

**The monetizable opportunity.** A SaaS package of assets, privacy-preserving liabilities and off-chain-obligation disclosure, mapped to GENIUS and MiCA attestation formats, sold to the roughly one hundred mid-tier exchanges and to the newly capitalized EM neobanks and custodians. It is a solid B2B business rather than a huge one, which is why it sits in the middle of the list.

### 8. A pluralistic identity router and a ZK-unlinkable layer for EU digital ID

**Source posts.** "Soulbound" (January 26, 2022), "What do I think about biometric proof of personhood?" (July 24, 2023) and "Does digital ID have risks even if it's ZK-wrapped?" (June 28, 2025) argue that one-per-person IDs, even ZK-wrapped, enable coercion and kill pseudonymity, and ask for "pluralistic identity": many overlapping issuers, wallets holding several credentials, apps accepting any of them ([soulbound](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2022/01/26/soulbound.html); [biometric](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2023/07/24/biometric.html); [zkid](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/06/28/zkid.html)).

**How developed it is.** Single-provider PoP is big and struggling to monetize: World reports ~450M sign-ups but **17–38.5M verified humans** depending on source, laid off staff in June 2026 in a "strategic reset" amid revenue struggles, and pivoted to charging applications ([Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/world-shifts-from-crypto-identity-experiment-to-enterprise-proof-of-humanity); [HRKatha](https://www.hrkatha.com/news/sam-altmans-tools-for-humanity-cuts-jobs-as-revenue-struggles-continue/)). Humanity Protocol carries a $1.1B valuation; Human Passport aggregates stamps for **2.18M verified accounts and 51.5M credentials** ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/vc-roundup-humanity-protocol-hits-unicorn-status-arch-labs-others-secure-major-funding/); [Holonym](https://human.tech/blog/holonym-foundation-quarterly-update-q2-2026)). ERC-7812's ZK identity registry is deployed on mainnet with no adoption metrics ([Ethereum Magicians](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-7812-zk-identity-registry/21624)). The router Vitalik described exists as a June 2026 IACR paper and one hobbyist repo, **not as a product** ([IACR 2026/333](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/333)). The EU's wallet, the biggest issuer of all, is heading for a miss: **~24 of 27 member states** are expected to blow the December 24, 2026 deadline, with bank acceptance obligations from July 2027 ([Brussels Signal](https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/09/eu-digital-identity-wallet-stalls-in-24-member-states-as-brussels-reopens-its-privacy-rulebook/); [Didit](https://didit.me/blog/amlr-eudi-wallet-acceptance/)).

**Why the gap remains.** Every provider wants to be the one issuer, and the regulatory text on unlinkability is still being reopened.

**The monetizable opportunity.** Demand is forming on the B2B "verify the human behind the agent" side: Proof's x401 (June, with Circle, OpenAI, Google, Okta contributing), World's AgentKit, Cloudflare's agent identities ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/26/proofs-x401-establishes-an-open-protocol-for-ai-agent-identity-and-authorization/)). A router that accepts World, Self, Rarimo, Passport and EUDI credentials behind one API, and a ZK presentation layer that makes EUDI credentials unlinkable ahead of the 2027 acceptance deadlines, are both unoccupied.

## Where VEIL sits in this ranking

VEIL, the reader's repository, is a direct build of idea #1 and of its sub-gap, the association-set provider: a shielded pool with Poseidon commitments, withdrawals proven against an approved association set (proof of innocence), notes encrypted to viewing keys for chosen auditors, a relayer for gasless proving, an ASP service for screening, and a Solana Token-2022 confidential-transfer prototype alongside the EVM contracts. The ranking was built from the market evidence before the repository was examined, and #1 would hold that position without VEIL; still, the reader should discount for the natural bias of seeing one's own project at the top.

Three caveats follow from the evidence. First, the gap in #1 is not primitives. Privacy Pools has the same proof-of-innocence design, Vitalik's co-authorship, a $3.5M seed and eighteen months on mainnet, and it sits at ~$9M of TVL; Railgun has working shielded transfers and earns ~$1M a quarter. VEIL's differentiation has to come from distribution (a wallet, neobank or payment processor that already has users), from a regulator-accepted disclosure story (viewing keys mapped to Travel Rule obligations, which Payy's analysts call a narrow line), and from an ASP that someone with compliance liability is willing to rely on. Second, the timing asymmetry is real and short: the US window opened on October 5, the EU's AMLR closes CASP-routed flows in July 2027, and self-custodial peer-to-peer transfers are the surviving lane, so VEIL's architecture choice between CASP-mediated and self-custodial flows is also a jurisdiction choice. Third, the proving-system risk is not theoretical: Aztec shipped two critical bugs in successive versions, and VEIL's circom withdrawal circuit is exactly the component where idea #5 (AI-assisted formal verification) and idea #1 intersect. VEIL also overlaps with #6 (its relayer and view-key handling are wallet-layer concerns that a Kohaku-based wallet would otherwise own) and could add #7's proof-of-solvency logic for the custodial counterparties it serves; but it does not correspond to #2, #3 or #4.

## Conclusion

The most useful thing the five-year record reveals is a consistent inversion: every layer Vitalik described as necessary scaffolding (smart accounts, attestations, proof-of-reserves, prediction exchanges) has been built and commoditized, often at unicorn scale, while the layer he described as the point (privacy, trust-minimization, non-financialized governance, defense-favoring hardware) has stayed at spec, alpha or pilot. The explanation is not technical difficulty; Privacy Pools, Kohaku, MACI and the keystore-rollup designs all work. It is that these layers remove rent rather than create it, so they attract grants rather than companies. The ideas that rank highest here are precisely the ones where a rent-creating business can be wrapped around a rent-removing primitive: a payment rail around a privacy pool, a vault around a liquidation-free synthetic, a policy product around a sandboxed agent, a creation API around open information markets.

For a builder, the practical reading is that the window on #1 is a nine-month regulatory arbitrage that began on October 5, 2026, and that the deciding asset is distribution, not cryptography. The second-order reading is that the quarter's loudest protocol news, EIP-8288's 99% cheaper private transactions and the Lean Ethereum roadmap, will make shielded stablecoin transfers nearly free at the base layer within the Hegota-to-Lean transition, which means the durable business in idea #1 is the compliance and disclosure layer (association sets, viewing-key policy, attestations) rather than the shielding itself. Teams that treat privacy as the product will be commoditized by the protocol; teams that treat compliant disclosure as the product will be the ones the protocol makes cheaper to run.
