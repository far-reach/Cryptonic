## Key Question 1: What has Vitalik published between July 1, 2026 and October 8, 2026?

### Takeaway
Exactly three blog posts appear on the site index for Jul 1 – Oct 8, 2026: "Obfuscation (Part II): Diamond iO" (Jul 28), "Obfuscation (Part III): Local Mixing" (Aug 21) and "The cryptographic world computer" (Sep 27); on Sep 27 he also released a full ~103k-word GPL-v3 science-fiction novel, *Snowmoon*, at vitalik.eth.limo/snowmoon. No post dated October 2026 exists on the index as of Oct 8; a Startup Fortune item describing an "October 6 blog post" is single-sourced and matches the April 2 LLM-setup post's content, so treat it as re-coverage, not a new post.

### Cited Findings — the Jul–Oct 2026 blog posts

**2026-07-28 — Obfuscation (Part II): Diamond iO — https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/07/28/obfuscation_part_ii_diamond_io.html**
- Second installment of his cryptographic-obfuscation series; a deep look at "diamond iO," a lattice-heavy construction that rests on less-tested assumptions than the mainstream line but targets "planetary" rather than "galactic" runtime — still infeasible today — [site index via search](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/07/28/obfuscation_part_ii_diamond_io.html); [Vitalik on X, Jul 28 2026](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2082132765886108082).
- Not [BUILDABLE] in itself: a cryptography explainer. The motivating application (from Part I) is a "trustless third party": obfuscated programs plus a blockchain enabling secret on-chain voting and other private computation — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406506/vitalik-buterin-calls-obfuscation-cryptographys-final-boss-but-says-current-approaches-remain-wildly-impractical); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/vitalik-says-obfuscation-and-blockchains-could-create-a-trustless-third-party/).

**2026-08-21 — Obfuscation (Part III): Local Mixing — https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/08/21/obfuscation_part_iii_local_mixing.html**
- Third approach to iO: discards lattices and elliptic curves in favor of ideas from hash-function design; technical content centers on reversible circuits (e.g., an "r57" gate that flips wire C unless A=0 and B=1) — [site index via search](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/08/21/obfuscation_part_iii_local_mixing.html); [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/vitalik-buterin-local-mixing-obfuscation/).
- Not [BUILDABLE]: cryptography tutorial. Press coverage of this post's content is thin.

**2026-09-27 — The cryptographic world computer — https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/09/27/the_cryptographic_world_computer.html**
- Vitalik's "attempt to express in somewhat concise terms the true meaning of basically everything planned to happen to Ethereum starting from the fork after Hegota"; "It's really not just a blockchain anymore. It's a hybrid architecture" of blockchain consensus plus modern cryptography — [Vitalik on X, Sep 27 2026](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2104160561374343176).
- Covers FOCIL, EIP-8288, lean consensus, state management, formal verification, mempool improvements including privacy, and obfuscation; Hegota (2027) is framed as Ethereum's last "normal" fork, with 4–8 s slots and 8–32 s finality targeted by 2030, nodes verifying via PeerDAS sampling + SNARK proofs instead of re-execution, and protocol-native social recovery, spending controls, sponsored gas and quantum-resistant signatures — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-09-27-its-really-not-just-a-blockchain-anymore-vitalik-buterin-maps-ethereums-path-to-2030-416953); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/379461/vitalik-buterin-outlines-cryptographic-world-computer-plan-for-ethereum); [Blockhead](https://www.blockhead.co/2026/09/28/vitalik-buterin-says-hegota-will-be-ethereums-last-normal-fork/).
- App-layer implication reported: not all computation should cost the same; apps split into independent, provable parts would cost less than monolithic step-by-step transactions — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-09-27-its-really-not-just-a-blockchain-anymore-vitalik-buterin-maps-ethereums-path-to-2030-416953). A reader summarized it as Ethereum's plan to "enable computation off chain with Eth verifying the result" — [X thread](https://x.com/cryptographicas/status/2104212366271390055).
- Classification: Ethereum-internal protocol roadmap, not flagged [BUILDABLE]; however it implicitly describes app patterns (off-chain provable computation with on-chain verification; "dependency claims" per transaction, see EIP-8288 below) that builders can target.

**2026-09-27 — *Snowmoon* (novel, 32 chapters, ~103,000 words) — https://vitalik.eth.limo/snowmoon/**
- Announced on X the same day as "Also this thing is done now" — [Vitalik on X](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2104216824258932907).
- Set in a world where cryptography and decentralized governance are everyday tools; ideological conflict between societies such as Veridia and the Arctic Empire; features zero-knowledge proofs, quadratic voting, privacy tech, and content-oriented tax mechanisms; released under GPL v3, with adaptations allowed if the production pipeline is open-sourced — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2292545); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/vitalik-buterin-publishes-sci-fi-novel-exploring-ethereum-s-vision).
- Background: on May 27, 2026 he announced on Farcaster that he would pause regular long-form essays to write science fiction on decentralized governance — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/vitalik-buterin-sci-fi-writing-pivot/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/vitalik-buterin-pivots-from-essays-to-sci-fi-novel-on-decentralized-governance/). He nevertheless kept publishing essays (Jun 29, Jul 28, Aug 21, Sep 27).
- Community builds already exist: Chinese translation repos, a full-cast audiobook/video pipeline, a role-play community chat (Tapaia), and a Farcaster mini-app reader — [GitHub guy-do-or-die/snowmoon](https://github.com/guy-do-or-die/snowmoon); [GitHub Tapaia](https://github.com/Tapaia/tapaia).
- Flag: [BUILDABLE-adjacent] — the novel is itself a GPL-licensed IP base he explicitly invites derivative works on (films, animations) provided pipelines are open-sourced.

### Cited Findings — Jan–Jun 2026 blog posts (context for the 2026 picture)
- The General index for 2026 runs: Apr 02 "My self-sovereign / local / private / secure LLM setup, April 2026"; May 18 ("A shallow dive into formal verification"); Jun 29 (Obfuscation Part I); Jul 28; Aug 21; Sep 27. Nothing is listed for Jan–Mar 2026 — [site index via search](https://vitalik.eth.limo/categories/general.html).
- **2026-04-02 — My self-sovereign / local / private / secure LLM setup, April 2026 — https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/04/02/secure_llms.html** [BUILDABLE]. Runs Qwen3.5:35B locally on an Nvidia 5090 laptop (~90 tok/s) via llama-server + llama-swap, in bubblewrap sandboxes rooted to whitelisted directories; open-sourced github.com/vbuterin/messaging-daemon wrapping signal-cli and email so the AI can read messages but every outbound message needs human approval ("human + LLM 2-of-2"); recommends wallet/AI tool builders cap autonomous spending at $100/day; cites Hiddenlayer data that ~15% of agent skills contain malicious instructions — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/363128/ethereu-founder-vitalik-buterin-details-private-secure-ai-setup); [mpost](https://mpost.io/vitalik-buterin-proposes-self-sovereign-ai-stack-to-protect-users-from-risks-of-ai-agents/); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/ethereum-s-vitalik-buterin-warns-of-ai-agent-security-risks-details-local-ai-setup). **What's missing:** a productized self-sovereign AI stack — local-model + sandbox + human-approval gating for outbound actions and wallet spend limits — that ordinary users can adopt without his hand-rolled setup.
- **2026-05-18 — A shallow dive into formal verification — https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/05/18/ (slug inferred)** [BUILDABLE]. Argues AI-assisted formal verification (machine-checkable proofs) is the "final form" of secure software development and the answer to AI bug-finding; names Claude, DeepSeek 4 Pro and Leanstral as models producing usable Lean proofs for Ethereum components, and Arklib (formally verified STARK) as an example; concedes proofs can miss assumptions, hardware bugs and adjacent attack surfaces — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/05/18/vitalik-buterin-says-ai-formal-verification-could-actually-make-crypto-much-more-secure); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/buterin-ai-formal-verification-security/); [Blockonomi](https://blockonomi.com/vitalik-buterin-ai-assisted-formal-verification-is-the-future-of-secure-software-development/). **What's missing:** AI-driven formal-verification tooling/services that make full FV of critical code (clients, provers, wallets, contracts) routine; "defining 'secure' is the hard part."
- **2026-06-29 — Obfuscation (Part I) — https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/06/29/ (slug inferred).** iO as cryptography's "final boss"; current constructions are polynomial but "literally galactic" in runtime; motivates secret on-chain voting and a "trustless third party" — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406506/vitalik-buterin-calls-obfuscation-cryptographys-final-boss-but-says-current-approaches-remain-wildly-impractical); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/vitalik-buterin-private-onchain-voting-obfuscation). Not buildable today (explicitly impractical).
