## Idea 1 — The "dream wallet" (keystore rollups, chain abstraction/EIL, Kohaku, Helios, EIP-7702, social recovery, funded startups)

### Takeaway
The dream wallet is **partially developed and highly fragmented**: the account layer (EIP-7702 ~27M delegated accounts; Safe 63M accounts/$27B) and intents (ERC-7683 in production via Across/Uniswap/Optimism) are mature, but the three pieces Vitalik specifically asked for — a live keystore rollup, a mainnet EIL, and a Helios-verifying consumer privacy wallet (Kohaku) — had **not shipped to mainnet as of Oct 2026**. Kohaku exists only as a v0.0.1-alpha SDK (May 25, 2026) with Ambire/breadcoop integrations "preparing"; no consumer wallet has shipped on it. No 2026 wallet startup funding round explicitly built around Vitalik's spec was found.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Keystore rollups / cross-L2 key management — verdict: largely undeveloped (no mainnet as of Oct 2026)**
- Vitalik's original "Dedicated minimal rollup for keystores" note proposes a rollup storing key-value data plus a key-change mechanism — [Vitalik HackMD](https://notes.ethereum.org/@vbuterin/minimal_keystore_rollup)
- Base's engineering blog says the team moved from committing keystore state to L1 toward a standalone ZK rollup called the Minimal KeyStore Rollup (MKSR), reported "fast finality, with changes visible across chains within a few minutes," and noted an EVM-based variant had a problem with signatures expiring early; the post does not state a mainnet deployment — [Base blog: Exploring the Keystore](https://blog.base.dev/exploring-the-keystore)
- A 2077 Research summary describes Base's MKSR as a based rollup posting its Merkle root to L1 with ZK-SNARK cross-chain sync, depth-64 indexed Merkle tree, five circuits, target recovery cost ~$1 — [2077 Research on X](https://x.com/2077Research/status/1907542004441198822)
- Axiom's keystore rollup was released as a private devnet settling to Sepolia (testnet), not mainnet — [Axiom Keystore](https://keystore.axiom.xyz/)
- Safe Foundation: Scroll and Base both published high-level specs; several teams building PoCs expected to be public-good implementations without a token; Safe/Scroll solutions "still in PoC stage and not yet applied to production" (article ~539 days old) — [Safe: Keystore Rollup](https://safefoundation.org/blog/keystore-rollup-smart-account-interoperability); [Scroll blog](https://scroll.io/blog/towards-the-wallet-endgame-with-keystore)

### Inferences
- The "dream wallet" stack has inverted: the generic account layer (7702/4337/Safe) is commoditized with tens of millions of accounts, while the trust-minimization layer Vitalik emphasized (keystore rollup, Helios verification, EIL) is where every project is still at spec/testnet/alpha. This is the layer with no revenue model and no token, which plausibly explains the slow progress.
- Kohaku's explicit positioning as "reference wallet for advanced users, not consumer" means the EF is deliberately leaving the consumer product gap open for third parties; Ambire is the de facto candidate but has not shipped.
- EIL's dependence on 4337 modules + 7702 delegations means its adoption is gated on wallet vendors, not on protocol readiness; the 63%-malicious-delegation finding (DelegProof) is a direct headwind to wallets opening 7702 delegation to new implementations.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Base MKSR or any keystore rollup has a public testnet/mainnet as of Oct 2026 (sources are 2024–25).
- No EIL audit completion, mainnet date, or Jul–Oct 2026 status update found.
- No Kohaku consumer-wallet launch, no Helios default-on consumer wallet, no 2026 Daimo Pay volume, no Base Account user count.
- No 2026 funding round found for a wallet explicitly implementing Vitalik's Dec 2024 wishlist.

## Idea 2 — Digital identity / proof of personhood (World, Self, Rarimo, Humanity, Billions, Human Passport, ERC-7812, EUDI Wallet, pluralistic identity, PoP for AI agents)

### Takeaway
PoP is **developed at the single-provider layer** (World ~38.5M verified humans per KuCoin/450M sign-ups per Biometric Update, but June 2026 layoffs and an enterprise-fee pivot; Humanity $1.1B valuation; Human Passport 2.18M verified accounts) and **active at the agent layer** (World AgentKit Mar 2026, Proof x401 Jun 2026, Cloudflare Wallets Aug 2026, x402 ~75M txs/$24M per 30 days in Sept 2026). The specific thing Vitalik asked for — a **pluralistic identity router** that accepts multiple independent PoP providers — **does not exist as a product**; Human Passport's stamp aggregation and a June 2026 IACR framework paper are the closest. The EUDI Wallet is heading for a mass deadline miss (24 of 27 states, Dec 24, 2026).
